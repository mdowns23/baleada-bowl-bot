📌 **#live-scores — How This Channel Works**

This channel posts **live score updates** every 60 seconds during NFL game windows and a **final scores summary** when the window closes.

**Game windows (ET):**
• Sunday — 1:00 PM through midnight
• Thursday Night — 8:15 PM through midnight
• Monday Night — 8:15 PM through midnight

---

**Slash Commands**

`/scores`
Shows the current week's matchup scores for all 6 games, sorted by matchup.

`/matchup [manager]`
Shows a full player-by-player breakdown of a manager's current matchup.
Example: `/matchup AKJenks`

`/standings`
Shows the full league standings sorted by record, then total points.
