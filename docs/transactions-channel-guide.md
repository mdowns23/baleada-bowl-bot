📌 **#transactions — How This Channel Works**

This channel posts an alert every time a transaction is **completed** in the league. The bot checks every 2 minutes, all week long.

**Transaction types posted:**
• 🔄 **Waiver Claim** — manager, player added/dropped, FAAB bid amount
• 🔄 **Free Agent Add** — manager, player added/dropped
• 🤝 **Trade** — both managers and what each side receives

---

**Slash Commands**

`/transactions`
Shows the 5 most recent completed transactions for the current week.

`/transactions [week]`
Shows the 5 most recent transactions for a specific week.
Example: `/transactions 4`

`/roster [manager]`
Shows a manager's full roster — starters and bench.
Example: `/roster Marquito49`
