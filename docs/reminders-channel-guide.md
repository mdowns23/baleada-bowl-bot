📌 **#reminders — How This Channel Works**

This channel enforces the **$5 ineligible starter penalty rule**.

**A player is ineligible if they are:**
• Listed as **Out, IR, PUP, or Suspended** at lineup lock
• Playing for a team that is **on bye** this week

---

**Warning Schedule**
The bot checks all starting lineups and posts a warning if any team has ineligible starters.

Warnings post at **12:00 PM ET** on:
• Wednesday
• Thursday
• Saturday
• Sunday

This gives you time to fix your lineup before it locks.

---

**Penalty Schedule**
After lineups lock, the bot checks again and records a **$5 penalty per ineligible player** still in the starting lineup.

Penalty checks run at:
• **Sunday 1:05 PM ET** — 1 PM games locked
• **Thursday 8:20 PM ET** — Thursday Night Football locked
• **Monday 8:20 PM ET** — Monday Night Football locked

---

**Slash Commands**

`/penalties`
Shows the full season penalty ledger — every team's total owed and the breakdown by week.
